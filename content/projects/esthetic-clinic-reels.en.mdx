## Overview

Short-form videos (reels) for an aesthetic clinic, with modern, elegant direction highlighting services and results.

## The challenge

Short-form social content has a few seconds to capture attention. In the aesthetics sector specifically, visual elegance is part of the message itself — poor content does more harm than good.

## Approach

**Fast pacing in the opening seconds** to stop the scroll before the viewer moves on.

**Elegant direction** reflecting the standard of service offered — visual quality here is a marketing message in itself.

**Formatting suited to vertical platforms** without cropping that unbalances the composition.

## What the work covered

Production of a marketing reels series, direction, editing, and colour grading.

## Technologies

Short-form video production · editing · colour grading · sound design
