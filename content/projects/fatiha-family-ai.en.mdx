## Overview

An AI-assisted family film expressing the meanings of Surat Al-Fatiha in a warm, cinematic visual style.

## The challenge

Religious content requires particular sensitivity: expressing meanings visually without overstepping, in a style that respects the text's sanctity while reaching a family audience.

## Approach

**A warm visual style** that speaks to a family setting and avoids didactic directness.

**Cinematic direction** in shot composition, lighting, and transitions, so the work reads as a film rather than a presentation.

**Content review** before production to ensure the meanings were expressed appropriately.

## What the work covered

Full AI-assisted film production, scene design, colour grading, editing, and sound design.

## Technologies

AI generation · cinematic direction · colour grading · sound design
