## Overview

Professional motion graphics videos for a dental clinic, with contemporary design and cinematic animation.

## The challenge

Medical content faces a dilemma: explaining procedures that may worry patients in a way that reassures rather than deters. Motion graphics solve this because they clarify without realistic imagery that might unsettle.

## Approach

**Clarification, not intimidation** — illustrations explain the procedure clearly while keeping the overall feel reassuring.

**Smooth animation and visual effects** that hold viewer attention in content that could otherwise feel technical.

**A consistent visual identity** across the videos so they read as one series rather than separate pieces.

## What the work covered

Design and production of motion graphics videos, animation, visual effects, and final editing.

## Technologies

Motion graphics · animation · visual effects · identity design
