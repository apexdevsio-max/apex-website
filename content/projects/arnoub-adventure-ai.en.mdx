## Overview

Turning the children's story "Arnoub's Paper Adventure" into a 2D animated video using AI techniques.

## The challenge

Turning written text into animation requires building a complete visual world: characters consistent across scenes, narrative pacing suited to children, and motion that serves the story rather than distracting from it.

## Approach

**Character consistency across scenes** — the hardest technical challenge in AI-assisted production, since the same character must stay recognisable in every shot.

**An appealing illustration style** aimed at the target age group.

**Narrative pacing** that follows the original story's structure rather than the tool's constraints.

## What the work covered

Converting the full story into an animated video, character and background design, animation, and final editing.

## Technologies

AI generation · 2D animation · character design · editing
