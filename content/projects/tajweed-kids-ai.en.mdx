## Overview

A series of educational videos explaining Quranic recitation rules (Tajweed) to children in simplified form, produced with AI techniques.

## The challenge

Teaching Tajweed rules to children requires a careful balance: simplification that makes the concept clear, with accuracy that leaves no room for error in religious content.

## Approach

**Visuals designed for the age group** that hold a child's attention without distracting from the content.

**Simplification without distortion** — each rule is explained with a clear example, with the content reviewed for accuracy before production.

**AI to accelerate visual production** while the educational content and sequencing remained human decisions.

## What the work covered

Production of an AI-assisted educational video series, character and scene design, and final editing.

## Technologies

AI generation · character design · educational editing · sound design
