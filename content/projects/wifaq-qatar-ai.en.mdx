## Overview

Introductory video content for Wifaq Qatar Foundation, produced with AI techniques to present its humanitarian mission supporting families.

## The challenge

Social foundations need content that conveys a humanitarian message sincerely without feeling like advertising. Traditional production for this kind of work is expensive in both time and budget.

## Approach

**AI as a production tool rather than a replacement for direction.** We used generative techniques to build the visual scenes while keeping narrative, message, and pacing as human decisions.

**A modern visual style** reflecting the foundation's humanitarian work and speaking to its local audience.

## What the work covered

Design and production of AI-assisted introductory videos, visual processing, and final editing ready for publication.

## Technologies

AI video generation · visual processing · editing · sound design
