# How Much Does an AI Customer Service Chatbot Cost? (2026 Guide)

**Adding an AI chatbot to your existing website or app costs $10,000–$40,000+, and a standalone AI customer-service product costs $40,000–$150,000+.** A scripted bot built as its own small app is a simple app at $5,000–$15,000. On top of the build, an AI bot has a monthly model bill: about $36–$216 for 3,000 conversations a month at today's list prices.

Ranges from our published rate card, as of November 2026. Which line you are on depends on two things: whether the bot needs to understand free-form questions, and whether it lives inside a product you already have.

**What moves an AI bot within $10,000–$40,000+:**

- **Lower end:** one channel (your website), answers drawn from a curated FAQ or knowledge base, handoff to a human by ticket or email.
- **Upper end:** lookups in your own systems (order status, bookings, CRM), more than one channel, testing across your customers' Arabic dialects, and an admin screen for your team to update the content.
- **Beyond the add-on:** once you need several channels, several languages, role-based permissions and audit logs as a product in its own right, you are in the AI-powered app tier.

## The two kinds of bot

**Rule-based bots** work from predefined menus and scripts: "press 1 for order status". They answer exactly what they were designed for and fail on anything outside the script. They are cheap and predictable, and enough if your customers' questions are repetitive and few.

**LLM-based bots** are built on a [large language model](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/resources/intro-llms). They understand free-form phrasing and answer from your own content, usually through retrieval from your documents ([RAG](/en/blog/rag-knowledge-base)). They cost more and need tuning and monitoring, but they handle questions you did not anticipate. This is what people usually mean by "AI chatbot" today.

| Criterion | Rule-based | LLM-based |
|---|---|---|
| Build cost (rate card) | Simple app, $5,000 – $15,000, or a subscription tool | AI integration, $10,000 – $40,000+ |
| Monthly running cost | Hosting or subscription only | Hosting plus model fees per message |
| Understands free-form phrasing | No | Yes |
| Answers from your documents | Only what is scripted | Yes |
| Answer predictability | Very high | Needs tuning and review |

## The monthly bill: model fees at current prices

An AI bot is billed per message, because every reply consumes tokens from the model provider. That cost starts at launch and does not end.

The worked example in [what AI costs to run](/en/blog/llm-running-costs) prices a support assistant handling **3,000 conversations a month, 6 model calls each**, with list prices read on 17 November 2026:

| Setup | Model fees per month |
|---|---|
| Mid-tier model (Claude Sonnet 5.5 or gpt-6.2-sol: $2 input / $10 output per million tokens), no caching | $216 |
| Same model, fixed instructions cached | $148 |
| 80% of calls on a small model (Claude Haiku 5.5 or gpt-6-luna: $0.10 / $0.50), 20% on the mid-tier model | $36 |

Prices are from [Anthropic's pricing page](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/about-claude/pricing) and [OpenAI's pricing page](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/pricing). Hosting, vector search for your documents and monitoring tools are billed by the providers you choose, according to usage. Someone on your team also needs time each week to read conversations and correct the content; that is staff time, not a licence.

> **A question to ask any vendor:** "Roughly what will each conversation cost me in model fees?" The answer should be a number with a calculation behind it.

## The Arabic-specific work

This is what separates a chatbot project in the Gulf from a generic one:

- **Dialects.** Customers write in Modern Standard Arabic, Gulf, Egyptian or Levantine Arabic, and mix them in one sentence. Quality varies by model and dialect, so **testing must cover your actual audience's dialects**, not MSA alone.
- **Arabizi.** Some users write Arabic in Latin letters and digits ("3ayez a3rf"). Check your own chat logs to see how many of your customers do, and test for it if they do.
- **Spelling variation.** Arabic is usually written without diacritics, and variations in hamza forms and taa marbuta must not break understanding or search.
- **Right-to-left rendering.** The chat widget needs correct right-to-left layout, including Arabic and English mixed in one message.
- **Token counts.** The same meaning can take a different number of tokens in Arabic, which changes the model bill; measure on real Arabic transcripts.
- **Accuracy over fluency.** A bot that answers in elegant Arabic with wrong information is worse than one that says "I don't know". **Always build in handoff to a human** when confidence drops.

**Data protection.** A bot that sends customers' messages to a model hosted abroad is transferring personal data. Under Saudi Arabia's [Personal Data Protection Law](https://misa.gov.sa/app/uploads/2025/08/PersonalDataProtectionLaw.pdf), Article 29 limits transfers outside the Kingdom to set purposes and requires, among other conditions, an adequate level of protection abroad and only the minimum data needed. Decide where the model runs and what you send it before the build; see the [PDPL compliance guide](/en/blog/pdpl-compliance-guide) and [data residency in the Gulf](/en/blog/data-residency-gulf).

## When does a bot pay for itself? Work it out

**Monthly saving = repetitive enquiries × share the bot resolves × cost of handling one enquiry**

An illustration with assumed inputs, not benchmarks: 2,000 enquiries a month, the bot resolves 60%, and each costs $3 to handle by a person.

`2,000 × 0.60 × $3 = $3,600 saved a month`

Against a $25,000 build (SAR 94,000 / AED 92,000 / QAR 91,000, inside the AI integration range) and an assumed $800 a month for model fees, hosting and review time, the net saving is about $2,800 a month, which repays the build in **about 9 months**.

Use your own numbers. If you do not know how many repetitive enquiries you receive, **measure that first**: at a few hundred a month, the same arithmetic rarely justifies an AI build.

## Cheaper alternatives

- **A well-written FAQ page** answers a good share of repetitive questions at almost no cost. Start here.
- **An off-the-shelf support tool** with a built-in bot, on a monthly subscription, if your channels and content are standard.
- **A scripted bot** as a simple app ($5,000–$15,000) if your questions are few and predictable.
- **A custom AI bot** earns its cost when enquiry volume is high, your content is specialised, or it must look things up in your own systems.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does it cost to add an AI chatbot to my existing website?

$10,000–$40,000+ as of November 2026 (SAR 37,500–150,000+). The lower end is one channel answering from a curated knowledge base; the upper end adds lookups in your own systems, more channels and dialect testing.

### When is a scripted bot enough, and what does it cost?

When your customers ask a small, stable set of questions that fit menus. Built as its own small app it is the simple tier at $5,000–$15,000; configured inside an off-the-shelf support tool it is a monthly subscription.

### What will the model fees be for my volume?

Multiply conversations by calls per conversation and by tokens per call, then by the provider's current prices. For 3,000 conversations a month that came to $36–$216 at 17 November 2026 prices, depending on model size and caching.

### Does sending customer chats to an AI provider abroad raise PDPL issues?

Yes, for personal data of people in Saudi Arabia. Article 29 of the PDPL allows transfers outside the Kingdom only for set purposes and under conditions including adequate protection abroad and minimum data. Settle hosting and data flows before building.

### How do I stop the bot from giving wrong answers?

Restrict it to answering from your approved content, set a confidence threshold below which it hands off to a person, and review conversation logs every week. Human handoff is a requirement, not an extra.

### Will the bot understand Gulf dialect and Arabizi?

Current models understand many dialects, but quality varies by model and dialect. Ask for testing on real messages in your customers' dialects, including Arabizi if your logs show it, before you sign off.
