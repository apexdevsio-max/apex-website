# Salla or Zid? A Practical Comparison

**For a conventional Saudi retail store, either Salla or Zid will work — so decide on price at your expected sales volume, the number of staff logins you need, whether your accounting and warehouse systems already connect, and how cleanly you can export your data.** Zid's published plans run from free to SAR 299 a month; Salla's paid plans are sold monthly or at a discount for annual billing. The rest of this guide turns those four tests into a decision you can make in a week.

Both are Saudi platforms built for this market, both handle Arabic natively rather than as a translation layer, and both offer local payment methods such as mada. Neither will fail a standard store on the fundamentals. Where they differ is in plan limits, developer access and how far you can bend the checkout.

If you already suspect a hosted platform is too restrictive for your model, read [Shopify or a Custom Store?](/en/blog/shopify-vs-custom-store) first. ## Plans and prices (read 17 November 2026)

**Zid** publishes four plans on its [pricing page](https://zid.sa/en/pricing/):

| Zid plan | Price | Staff logins | Custom domain | Advanced payment methods | Invoice and tax settings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | Free forever | 1 | No | No | No |
| Rise | SAR 99 / month | 1 | Yes | Yes | No |
| Growth | SAR 299 / month | 5 | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Pro | On request | 20 | Yes | Yes | Yes |

Zid's page has a monthly/annual toggle but showed no annual figures, and its "is there a commission on sales?" answer was not visible when we read it — ask Zid to confirm both in writing.

**Salla** sells its plans through its [plans page](https://salla.com/en-sa/plans). Salla's own listing showed Plus at SAR 990 a year and Pro at SAR 2,990 a year on annual billing (against SAR 3,588 a year for Pro paid monthly, i.e. SAR 299 a month), with Special, its enterprise tier, priced on request. We could not load that page directly on 17 November 2026, so open it and confirm the current figures before you subscribe.

**What the numbers mean in practice:**

- **Testing demand with no budget:** Zid Starter costs nothing and its pricing page lists unlimited products, orders and customers — but no custom domain and no advanced payment methods.
- **A real store on your own domain:** the entry paid tiers on both platforms sit around SAR 99 a month.
- **More than one person running the store:** on Zid you need Growth (SAR 299 a month, five logins). Check the login limit on the Salla plan you are considering.
- **Paying annually:** Salla publishes an annual price that works out cheaper than twelve monthly payments; ask Zid for its annual figure.

Payment processing fees are charged on top of the subscription on any platform, so ask each for its mada, card and buy-now-pay-later rates before you compare totals. The [Gulf payment gateways guide](/en/blog/gulf-payment-gateways) explains what each method costs to add.

## Where they actually differ

### App and integration ecosystem

Both run an app marketplace: Salla's developer documentation describes publishing apps to the Salla App Store, and Zid's partner documentation describes public apps listed in the Zid App Market. The number of apps matters less than whether **your** systems are there.

**Check before choosing:** list the systems your business already runs — accounting, warehouse, CRM, shipping — and confirm each has a working integration on the platform you are considering. This single check predicts more future frustration than any feature comparison, because the integration you need usually becomes obvious in month four, not at signup.

### Developer access and APIs

[Salla's Merchant API documentation](https://docs.salla.dev/) is public and lists OAuth 2.0 authentication, webhooks for store events, and endpoints for products, orders, customers and invoices under a single base URL. [Zid's developer documentation](https://docs.zid.sa/) covers app types (public, private, shipping and subscription apps), the review process and webhook logs in its Partner Dashboard.

If you plan to connect a mobile app, an ERP or a custom dashboard later, ask your developer to confirm the exact endpoints they need on each platform before you commit. See our [e-commerce app cost guide](/en/blog/ecommerce-app-cost) for what a companion app involves.

### Checkout and conversion control

Both platforms restrict checkout changes more than a custom store would. If your business needs something unusual at checkout — a required field for B2B customers, a delivery-date picker, shipping rules by product type — verify it is possible on a trial store before signing up. Zid limits CSS customisation to Growth and above, so price that in if your design depends on it.

### Tax invoices

Saudi e-invoicing (FATOORAH) has applied to VAT-registered taxpayers since [4 December 2021, with the integration phase rolling out in waves from 1 January 2023](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Introduction/Pages/What-is-e-invoicing.aspx). The obligation is yours, not the platform's. On Zid, invoice and tax settings start at Growth; confirm which Salla plan includes the invoicing features you need.

## Which fits which store

| Your situation | Leans toward |
|---|---|
| First store, testing demand with no budget | **Zid Starter** — free, unlimited products and orders, no custom domain |
| Small store on its own domain, one person running it | **Either entry tier** (around SAR 99 a month) — choose after trialling both checkouts on a phone |
| Two to five staff need their own logins | **Zid Growth** (SAR 299 a month, five logins), or the Salla plan whose login limit fits |
| You want to pay annually for a discount | **Salla** — its annual prices are published; ask Zid for an annual quote |
| You have a developer and need deep API access | **Salla**, on the strength of its public Merchant API reference — but have your developer check Zid's endpoints for your specific use case |
| Selling mainly outside Saudi Arabia | **Neither** — consider Shopify |
| Unusual checkout or pricing rules | **Neither** — you need a custom store; see [Shopify or Custom](/en/blog/shopify-vs-custom-store) |
| Wholesale or B2B with negotiated pricing | **Usually neither** — both are built for retail |

If your store is conventional Saudi retail, the table usually settles it in one or two rows. If your store is unusual, the question is not Salla or Zid — it is whether a hosted platform suits you at all.

## The question that matters more than the choice

**How do you get your data out?**

Almost nobody asks this at signup; everybody asks it eventually. Before committing, confirm:

- Can you export your full product catalogue, including images and variants?
- Can you export customer records and complete order history?
- Do you keep your domain, or is it registered through the platform?
- What happens to your data if you stop paying?

The answers decide whether you are a customer or a hostage. Get the export scope in writing before you have three years of business inside either platform. The same principle applies to any hosted platform and is covered in [Template or Custom Design?](/en/blog/template-vs-custom-design).

## How to decide in a week

1. **Trial both.** Build the same three products on each and take a test order through to completion.
2. **Test the checkout on a phone.** Most of your customers will buy on mobile.
3. **Contact support with a real question** on both, and note how long the reply takes and whether it answers what you asked.
4. **Check your required integrations** exist on each.
5. **Price both at next year's volume,** not today's — including the plan you will need when you add staff.

Whichever wins on those five, choose it and stop researching. The difference between them is smaller than the cost of another month spent deciding.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Salla better than Zid for a first store?

Neither is better in general. If you want to start at zero cost, Zid's Starter plan is free forever with unlimited products and orders, but it has no custom domain or advanced payment methods. Once you need your own domain, the entry paid tiers on both platforms cost about the same, so trial both and choose on checkout speed and integrations.

### Which is cheaper at SAR 299 a month?

At that level Zid Growth gives five staff logins plus invoice and tax settings; Salla Pro was listed at the same SAR 299 when paid monthly, or SAR 2,990 a year on annual billing. Compare what each includes at that price — logins, invoice settings, CSS control — rather than the headline figure.

### Can I move my store from Salla to Zid, or back, later?

You can export and re-import products, but migration is disruptive: URLs change, customer accounts do not transfer cleanly, and order history often does not follow. Plan 301 redirects for every product URL and confirm export completeness before you commit, not after.

### Do Salla and Zid issue ZATCA-compliant invoices for me?

The e-invoicing obligation sits with you as the VAT-registered seller. Both platforms provide invoicing features, but on Zid the invoice and tax settings start at the Growth plan; check which Salla plan includes them and confirm the invoices meet your current ZATCA phase before you go live.

### Should I use Shopify instead of Salla or Zid?

If you sell mainly outside the region, Shopify's international reach is stronger. For a store selling mostly inside Saudi Arabia, note that Shopify's own payments service is not offered there, so Shopify charges an extra third-party transaction fee on each sale. See [Shopify or a Custom Store?](/en/blog/shopify-vs-custom-store).

### When should I leave Salla or Zid for a custom store?

When the platform's limits start costing you sales: a checkout you cannot build, pricing rules the platform does not support, or an ERP integration its API cannot carry. A custom store costs $20,000–$65,000 (about SAR 75,000–244,000) to build as of November 2026, so the trigger is a capability you need, not a wish to save on subscriptions.

### Can I connect a mobile app to my Salla or Zid store?

Yes — both expose APIs a mobile app can read products, stock and orders from. Confirm the endpoints you need exist before building; a full store app sits in the $20,000–$65,000 range on our rate card, as of November 2026.
