# App Development in Abu Dhabi: Pricing and Selection Guide (2026)

Many treat Abu Dhabi and Dubai as one market, and that mistake costs money. **Abu Dhabi has a different character**: more tied to government, energy, and sovereign funds, less crowded with service providers, with longer decision cycles but larger budgets.

This guide gives you realistic 2026 pricing, what distinguishes Abu Dhabi projects, and regulatory requirements that directly affect your budget.

## Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM): what you need to know

If your company is licensed in **Abu Dhabi Global Market**, you operate under an independent legal framework — not the general federal regime.

[ADGM](https://www.adgm.com/) is a financial free zone operating under its own legal and regulatory framework **built on English common law**, with independent courts and full foreign ownership. It has an independent data protection framework closer to European standards than to the federal regime.

**Why does this matter for an app project?**

- **Consent requirements and user rights** are more detailed — and designed into the architecture from the start, not added later.
- **Cross-border data transfer restrictions** need architectural handling, especially if you use external cloud services.
- **Fintech startups** gain access to a regulatory sandbox (RegLab) — a genuine advantage if your product is financial.

> **Ask your provider:** "have you built for an entity licensed in ADGM or DIFC before?" The difference between a free zone framework and the federal regime is not a legal footnote but a design decision that affects cost. And always verify your position with your legal advisor, as frameworks change quickly — updates issued in 2025 and 2026 touch several activities.

## Requirements that add to your budget

**Payment gateways.** Gateways operating in the UAE — such as Network International, Telr, and PayTabs — alongside Apple Pay and buy-now-pay-later options. Budget **$1,500 – $4,000 per gateway**.

**Localisation and RTL support.** Although most residents are not Arabic speakers, government entities and the local audience expect a correct Arabic version — effectively mandatory for government projects. **$2,000 – $8,000**.

**Electronic invoicing.** The UAE is proceeding with a federal e-invoicing rollout. If your app issues invoices, ask about your provider's readiness for this requirement **before** the build.

**Store fees.** Apple: $99 annually · Google: a one-time $25 registration fee.

1. **A live portfolio** — working store links, not screenshots.

5. **Complete ownership** — code, store accounts, and domain in your name.
6. **Post-launch support** — warranty period and annual maintenance cost in writing.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does app development cost in Abu Dhabi?**

An MVP app ranges from $15,000 to $35,000 through a local firm, or $8,000 to $25,000 with a regional team. A mid-size business app reaches $80,000 locally. Pricing tracks Dubai closely and runs 15–25% above Riyadh.

**What is the difference between the Abu Dhabi and Dubai markets?**

Abu Dhabi is more tied to government and energy, with longer decision cycles, larger budgets, and higher documentation requirements. Dubai is faster, more diverse, and far more crowded with providers. Pricing is comparable, but project character differs fundamentally.

**What are the ADGM requirements?**

Abu Dhabi Global Market is a financial free zone with an independent legal framework built on English common law, and a data protection framework closer to European standards than the federal regime. If your company is licensed there, consent requirements, user rights, and data transfer restrictions are designed into your app's architecture from the start.

**Do I need an Arabic version of my app?**

Yes in most cases, and effectively mandatory for government projects. Although most UAE residents are not Arabic speakers, official entities and the local audience expect correct Arabic. Professional localisation includes mirrored RTL layouts, not merely translated text.

**How long does app development take?**

MVP: 6–10 weeks. Mid-size app: 3–5 months. Enterprise platform: 9–18 months. **Government projects in Abu Dhabi run longer** due to multiple review and approval cycles — add a time buffer to your planning.

**Should I choose a local firm or a remote team?**

For government projects or those requiring frequent presence, a local firm suits better. For well-scoped projects, a regional team working in a nearby time zone delivers the same quality at 30–45% lower cost. What matters is that the provider understands the regulatory framework applying to your entity.
