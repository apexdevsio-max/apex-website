# Shopify vs a Custom Store: The Real Three-Year Calculation

**Start on a hosted platform — Shopify, Salla or Zid — unless you already need something it cannot do: a non-standard checkout, negotiated or wholesale pricing, or a deep ERP integration.** A custom store costs $20,000–$65,000 (about SAR 75,000–244,000) to build as of November 2026, and over three years it rarely wins on price alone. It wins when the platform starts blocking how you sell, or when per-sale fees grow larger than a build you would own.

This guide is the decision framework. The full line-by-line three-year cost table for Saudi stores lives in our [e-commerce store cost guide for Saudi Arabia](/en/blog/ecommerce-development-cost-saudi); here we only use its totals.

---

## The two options in one sentence each

**Hosted platform ([Shopify](https://www.shopify.com/pricing), [Salla](https://salla.com/en-sa/plans), [Zid](https://zid.sa/en/pricing/)):** you rent a pre-built store, start selling within days, and pay a permanent subscription plus, on some platforms and plans, a fee on every sale.

**Custom store:** you own the platform outright. A much higher upfront cost and 15–20% of the build cost every year for maintenance, but no platform subscription, no platform fee on sales, and no ceiling on what the checkout can do.

If your answer is "custom", our [web development team](/en/services/web-development) builds stores on that model; if it is "hosted", the rest of this guide still tells you when to revisit the decision.

---

## The decision in four questions

Work through these in order. The first "yes" settles it.

**1. Does your sales model break the platform?**
Subscriptions with complex rules, negotiated B2B prices per customer, quote-based ordering, or a checkout step the platform will not let you add. If yes → custom store, or at least a semi-custom open-source build. No amount of saved subscription fees compensates for a checkout that cannot take the order.

**2. Do you need two-way, real-time integration with an ERP or warehouse system?**
Stock, pricing and orders flowing both ways every few minutes, not a nightly export. If the platform's API and app store cannot carry it reliably → custom. If an existing connector covers it → stay hosted.

**3. Do per-sale fees exceed what a build would cost you over three years?**
This is the only cost line that grows with your success. Work it out with the formula below. If the fees are higher → custom earns its cost.

**4. Have you proved the product sells?**
If not → hosted, without exception. Building an expensive custom store before you have real sales data is the most common way to lose a year.

## The three-year calculation, in one formula

A custom store's three-year cost is the build plus three years of maintenance:

`custom 3-year cost = build cost × (1 + 3 × 15–20%)`

At the bottom of our rate card ($20,000 build), that is **about $29,000–$32,000** (SAR 108,800–120,000) over three years. Our [Saudi e-commerce cost guide](/en/blog/ecommerce-development-cost-saudi) sets this beside a hosted store's subscriptions line by line.

A hosted store's cost that grows with sales is the platform's fee on each transaction:

`hosted 3-year fees = annual sales × platform fee % × 3`

Card and mada processing fees from the payment provider apply to both kinds of store, so leave them out of the comparison — they cancel.

### Why Shopify's fee matters in Saudi Arabia

[Shopify Payments](https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/supported-countries) is available in the UAE but **not in Saudi Arabia or Qatar**. Stores there must use a third-party payment provider, and Shopify's [pricing page](https://www.shopify.com/pricing) charges a transaction fee on every sale made that way:

| Shopify plan | Third-party transaction fee | Annual sales at which three years of fees ≈ $29,000–$32,000 |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | 2% | about $500,000 (SAR 1,875,000) |
| Grow | 1% | about $1,000,000 (SAR 3,750,000) |
| Advanced | 0.6% | about $1,700,000 (SAR 6,375,000) |
| Plus | 0.2% | about $5,000,000 (SAR 18,750,000) |

Below those volumes the fee alone does not justify a custom build. Above them, a store you own starts to pay for itself — before counting the control it gives you. Moving up a Shopify plan to cut the fee is the cheaper first step; compare that plan's subscription against the fee it saves.

Salla and Zid price mainly by subscription. Zid's published plans run from free to SAR 299 a month, so at those prices the subscription will rarely be the reason to leave; questions 1 and 2 will be.

## What the calculation does not show

**In favour of a hosted platform:**
- **Speed** — you sell within days rather than months. In a competitive market, a three-month delay is a real cost.
- **Shared infrastructure** — hosting, security patches and checkout uptime are the platform's job, not yours.
- **Local fit (Salla and Zid)** — Arabic built in, local payment methods such as mada, and support in Saudi business hours.

**In favour of a custom store:**
- **Ownership** — nobody can raise prices or change the terms on you.
- **Performance** — a store carrying only what you need can be faster, which affects conversion and search; insist on measurable Core Web Vitals targets.
- **Flexibility** — unconventional models (complex subscriptions, negotiated pricing, wholesale) are possible.
- **Deep integration** — connecting to an existing ERP or accounting system is far easier.

## Salla and Zid vs Shopify — for the Saudi market

| Criterion | Salla / Zid | Shopify |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic and right-to-left layout | Built in | Depends on the theme; test it in Arabic before buying |
| Payments in Saudi Arabia | Local methods such as mada through the platform | Third-party provider required; 0.2–2% Shopify fee per sale on top |
| E-invoicing ([FATOORAH](https://zatca.gov.sa/en/E-Invoicing/Introduction/Pages/What-is-e-invoicing.aspx)) | Invoice features in the platform (on Zid, from the Growth plan) | Through apps; confirm the app meets your ZATCA phase |
| Support | Arabic, Saudi business hours | Global, mostly English |
| Selling outside the region | Limited | Strong |

**The verdict:** if you sell mainly inside Saudi Arabia, a local platform means less friction and no per-sale platform fee to model. If you target buyers abroad, Shopify is stronger — and in the UAE, where Shopify Payments is available, the third-party fee question disappears. To choose between the two local platforms, see [Salla or Zid?](/en/blog/salla-vs-zid).

## The middle path: an open-source store

Between renting and building from scratch sits **WooCommerce or another open-source store your team shapes**.

- **Cost on our rate card:** priced as a mid-size company website ($8,000–$25,000, SAR 30,000–93,800) plus $1,500–$5,000 (SAR 5,600–18,800) for each local payment gateway, as of November 2026.
- **No platform subscription and no platform fee on sales.**
- **The drawback:** you own the maintenance and security updates, and performance degrades as plugins accumulate.

It suits a store that has outgrown a hosted platform's checkout but does not need a fully custom build.

## Frequently asked questions

### What signs show my store has outgrown Shopify, Salla or Zid?

Three signs: you are building workarounds to take a normal order in your business model; your ERP or warehouse needs real-time, two-way sync the platform's API cannot carry; or three years of per-sale platform fees now exceed the cost of a store you would own. Any one is enough to start scoping a custom build.

### Do I lose my products, customers and rankings when I leave a hosted platform?

Products, customers and orders can be exported from most platforms. What is hard to move is **URLs and search ranking** — map a 301 redirect from every old product and category URL to its new one before you switch, or you lose the ranking you earned.

### Will a custom store be faster than my Shopify store?

Usually, because it carries only what you need. But a poorly built custom store can be slower than a well-tuned hosted one. Make measurable Core Web Vitals targets part of the contract rather than accepting promises.

### What does a custom store cost to run each year after launch?

Plan on 15–20% of the build cost a year for maintenance and security updates — $3,000–$13,000 (SAR 11,300–48,800) for a $20,000–$65,000 store, as of November 2026 — plus hosting and your payment provider's processing fees. New features you request later are extra.

### Is it smarter to launch on Shopify and rebuild custom later?

For most projects, yes. You test the market cheaply, collect real sales data, and then write the custom store's requirements from evidence rather than assumptions.

### Does Shopify charge extra for a store based in Saudi Arabia?

Not as a surcharge, but Shopify Payments is not available in Saudi Arabia, so you must use a third-party provider and Shopify adds its third-party transaction fee — 2% on Basic, 1% on Grow, 0.6% on Advanced, 0.2% on Plus — on top of that provider's own processing fee.

## The bottom line

A hosted platform is the right first store for almost everyone. Move to a custom store when your sales model, your integrations or your per-sale fees force the issue — and use the formula above, not a feeling, to tell which.
