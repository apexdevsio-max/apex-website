# Web Design Company in Dubai: A Complete Guide to Choosing and Pricing

Dubai is one of the most crowded markets for web service providers in the region — which cuts both ways. There are plenty of options, but telling a capable firm apart from one that is simply good at marketing itself is considerably harder.

This guide gives you realistic pricing for the Dubai market in 2026, specific UAE requirements that affect your budget, and practical evaluation criteria that reveal the difference before you pay.

---

## Expected pricing in Dubai — 2026

The Dubai market prices above the regional average, driven by high operating costs rather than necessarily higher quality.

| Website type | Dubai agency | Remote regional team |
|---|---|---|
| Landing page | $1,500 – $4,000 | $800 – $2,500 |
| Brochure site (5–8 pages) | $4,000 – $12,000 | $1,500 – $8,000 |
| Mid-size company site | $12,000 – $35,000 | $8,000 – $25,000 |
| Bilingual site with blog | $18,000 – $45,000 | $12,000 – $30,000 |

*Indicative estimates based on Apex project experience in this market as of 2026. Actual cost depends on scope, integrations, and the level of customisation your project requires.*

**The real difference:** a local Dubai agency offers physical presence and direct market familiarity. A remote regional team delivers the same technical quality at 30–45% lower cost. ## Requirements specific to the UAE market

These are not cosmetic details — ignoring them means a site that fails its audience or breaches regulation.

**Electronic invoicing.** The UAE is proceeding with a federal e-invoicing rollout, meaning any platform issuing invoices will need technical compliance. If your site sells or issues invoices, ask your provider about their readiness for this requirement before the build, not after.

**Genuine Arabic localisation.** Most UAE residents are not Arabic speakers, but government entities, large corporates, and the local audience expect a correct Arabic version. Machine translation is detected immediately and undermines credibility. Professional localisation adds 25–40% to cost and includes mirrored RTL layouts, not merely translated text.

**Local payment gateways.** If the site accepts payments, integration with gateways operating in the UAE — such as Network International, Telr, and PayTabs — alongside Apple Pay and buy-now-pay-later options, is now expected by UAE buyers. Budget $1,500–$4,000 per gateway.

**Jurisdiction and compliance.** The free zone your company operates in affects your data requirements. Companies registered in the **Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC)** or **Abu Dhabi Global Market (ADGM)** fall under independent data protection frameworks built on English common law and closely aligned with European GDPR standards — frameworks more detailed than the federal regime. If your company is licensed in either, the privacy, consent, and data storage requirements on your website are not optional.

> **Important note:** UAE regulatory frameworks change quickly, and updates issued in 2025 and 2026 touch contracting activities and invoicing. Verify your company's current position with your legal advisor before relying on any general guide — including this one.

## Seven criteria for choosing a firm

### 1. A live portfolio, not screenshots

Ask for links to sites actually published and open them yourself. Check their speed on your phone, and their Arabic version if one exists. Images in a slide deck prove nothing.

### 2. Who actually does the work?

Many Dubai agencies subcontract execution to teams outside the country. That is not inherently a flaw — but concealing it means you pay an extra margin without knowing. Ask directly.

### 3. Performance as a measurable commitment

Ask for an explicit commitment to [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) standards. A slow site loses visitors and falls in search results no matter how beautiful it is. This is an objective criterion you can verify after delivery.

### 4. Search optimisation from the foundation

Ask: does the quote include the SEO basics — titles, descriptions, structured data, and a sitemap? Adding these later costs more than building them into the foundation.

### 5. Complete ownership

Code, domain, hosting, and analytics accounts — all in your name. **Verify the domain is registered specifically in your name**, as some providers register it in theirs, making a later move nearly impossible.

### 6. Post-launch support

What is the warranty period? What does annual maintenance cost? A website needs regular security updates, and agreeing on this upfront is cheaper than negotiating at the first problem.

### 7. References you can speak to

Ask to contact two former clients, and ask: was the project delivered on schedule? How did they handle change requests?

## Five warning signs

- **A price far below market** — usually means a ready-made template with light edits, or a misunderstood scope you will pay for in revisions.
- **"A website in 48 hours"** — only possible with a stock template and no real content.
- **Refusal to register the domain in your name** — a direct red flag, not an administrative detail.
- **No mention of performance or SEO in the quote** — means a beautiful site nobody finds.
- **A portfolio with no live links** — no evidence anything actually launched.

## Frequently asked questions

### How much does web design cost in Dubai?

A professional brochure site in Dubai ranges from $4,000 to $12,000 through a local agency, or $1,500 to $8,000 with a remote regional team. A mid-size company site reaches $35,000 locally. The difference reflects Dubai operating costs rather than necessarily higher technical quality.

### Should I choose a Dubai agency or a remote team?

If you need frequent in-person meetings and a local presence, a local agency suits you better. If technical quality and cost are what matter, a regional team working in a nearby time zone delivers the same result at 30–45% lower cost. What actually matters is time-zone overlap and market understanding, not the postal address.

### How long does building a website take?

Landing page: 1–2 weeks. Brochure site: 2–5 weeks. Mid-size company site: 6–12 weeks. Custom platform: 4–8 months. Client delays in supplying content and images are the most common cause of overrun.

### Do I need an Arabic version for my UAE website?

Yes in most cases. Although most residents are not Arabic speakers, government entities, large corporates, and the local audience expect a correct Arabic version. Skipping it means losing an influential segment, particularly for projects targeting the government or enterprise sector.

### What are the annual costs after launch?

Domain, hosting, maintenance, and backups: between $700 and $4,500 per year depending on site size. Any quote that omits these items is incomplete.

### Is a website enough, or do I need an app too?

A responsive website is sufficient for most cases of presenting services, collecting enquiries, and selling. An app earns its cost when you need push notifications, offline operation, or repeat usage by the same customer.
