# Educational App Development Cost: From MVP to Full Platform (2026)

Digital education is among the fastest-growing sectors in the Gulf, driven by substantial government investment in educational digital transformation. But "educational app" covers entirely different products — from an app showing recorded courses to a complete school platform managing attendance, grades, and parents.

**The short answer: a basic course app costs $15,000 – $35,000. An integrated education platform runs $50,000 to $120,000+.**

---

## First: which type do you need?

| Type | What it does | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Course app | Recorded lessons, simple quizzes | $15,000 – $35,000 |
| Interactive app | + exercises, progress tracking, certificates, learning games | $35,000 – $70,000 |
| School platform | + attendance, grades, parents, timetables | $50,000 – $120,000 |
| Corporate training platform | + learning paths, compliance reports, permissions | $60,000 – $150,000 |

**The most common mistake:** building a complete school platform before verifying that the content itself attracts learners. Start with the smallest version that proves people **complete** your lessons, not merely enrol in them.

## Ongoing cost: video is the decisive item

An educational app differs from others in that **its operational cost rises in direct proportion to its success**. Every new learner watching video costs you money.

| Item | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| Video hosting and streaming | $200 – $3,000 |
| Content storage | $50 – $500 |
| Servers and database | $100 – $800 |
| Maintenance and updates | $800 – $3,000 |

**Total: $1,150 – $7,300 per month** depending on active learners and content volume.

> **A practical tip that saves thousands:** use a specialised streaming platform rather than building your own streaming infrastructure. The upfront cost difference is substantial, quality is better, and scaling is automatic.

## Protecting minors' data: the item you cannot defer

If your app serves students under the age of majority, you are handling **the most sensitive category of data**.

**In Saudi Arabia**, the Personal Data Protection Law has been fully enforced since September 2024 under [SDAIA](https://sdaia.gov.sa/), covering: consent as the lawful basis for processing, enhanced protection for sensitive data, breach notification within 72 hours, rights of access, correction and deletion, and restrictions on transferring data outside the Kingdom. **Minors' data requires guardian consent**, which means designing a verifiable guardianship mechanism rather than a checkbox.

**Design requirements that follow:**

- A documented, auditable guardian consent mechanism.
- Restricting data collection to the genuine minimum.
- No behavioural advertising targeted at minors.
- Strict communication controls if the app allows user-to-user interaction.

Budget **$2,000 – $6,000** for baseline compliance. And if you are targeting public schools, verify the relevant education authority's requirements before building — institutional requirements are often stricter than the general law.

## Revenue models and their development impact

Your revenue model determines which features you need to build, so decide it **before** development, not after:

- **Monthly/annual subscription** — needs subscription management, renewals, and failed payment handling: **+$4,000 – $10,000**.
- **One-time course purchase** — simpler: **+$3,000 – $7,000**.
- **Free with paid content** — needs precise entitlement logic: **+$3,000 – $8,000**.
- **Enterprise sales (B2B)** — needs an admin panel, seats, and reporting: **+$8,000 – $20,000**.

**An important note:** in-app subscriptions are subject to app store commission (up to 30%, reduced in certain cases). This materially affects your pricing — factor it into your financial model from the start.

## How do you raise completion rates?

The real problem with educational apps is not downloads but **completion**. Completion rates in digital learning are low generally, and these are the highest-impact practices:

1. **Short lessons** — 5–10 minutes substantially outperforms hour-long lectures.
2. **Visible progress tracking** — a completion bar drives finishing.
3. **Smart reminders** — not annoying daily ones, but tied to when a learner lapses.
4. **A certificate on completion** — an effective incentive, particularly in professional training.
5. **Offline download** — removes the network barrier entirely.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a simple educational app cost?**

A basic course app showing recorded lessons with simple quizzes costs between $15,000 and $35,000 and takes 3 to 5 months. Adding gamification, progress tracking, and certificates raises it to the $35,000–$70,000 range.

**What is the difference between a course app and a school platform?**

A course app delivers content to an individual learner. A school platform manages a multi-party relationship — student, teacher, parent, administration — with attendance, grades, and timetables. The latter is far more complex, starts at $50,000, and carries higher data protection requirements.

**What does it cost to operate monthly?**

Between $1,150 and $7,300 monthly depending on active learners, with video hosting and streaming the largest item ($200–$3,000). This cost rises in direct proportion to your growth — every new learner watching video adds to it.

**What are the requirements for protecting student data?**

Minors' data is among the most sensitive categories. Saudi PDPL requires guardian consent, minimising collection, breach notification within 72 hours, and restrictions on transfers outside the Kingdom. Budget $2,000–$6,000 for baseline compliance, and verify the education authority's requirements if targeting schools.

**How do I raise course completion rates?**

Short lessons (5–10 minutes) have the highest impact, followed by visible progress tracking, reminders tied to learner lapses, a completion certificate, and offline download. Start with short lessons — cheapest to implement and largest in effect.

**Should I start with an app or a web platform?**

Start with a responsive web platform in most cases: 40–60% cheaper, faster to deliver, and it avoids store commission on subscriptions. Move to an app when you need offline download, push notifications, or your numbers prove your audience prefers mobile. See our [website design cost guide](/en/blog/website-design-cost).
