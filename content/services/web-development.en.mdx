# Web Development

Your website is the first impression a client forms of your company — and often the last, if it is slow or unclear. Half of visitors leave when loading exceeds three seconds. That is not a marketing exaggeration; it is a measured figure from real web performance data.

We build sites that are fast, clear, search-ready, and designed around one commercial objective: getting the visitor to contact you.

---

## What actually makes a website work commercially

**Speed.** Every additional second of load time measurably reduces conversion. We build with Next.js using pre-rendered pages, automatic image optimisation, and minimal JavaScript shipped to the browser.

**Clarity.** A visitor must understand what you offer and how to reach you within five seconds. A beautiful design that fails to communicate is a commercial failure.

**Search visibility.** A site nobody can find is an expensive digital brochure. We build the SEO foundation from day one rather than bolting it on later.

**Trust.** Real work, clear contact details, and content that demonstrates expertise — this is what converts a hesitant visitor into a client.

## Arabic and RTL: not a translation layer

Most "Arabic" websites are translated English websites — the layout does not mirror, typography is untuned, numbers and dates use foreign formatting, and letter spacing makes the text tiring to read.

We treat Arabic as a first-class language: complete layout mirroring, locally hosted Arabic typefaces (not slow third-party servers), and correct hreflang tags telling Google that two versions of each page exist — which prevents your Arabic and English pages from competing against each other for the same ranking.
