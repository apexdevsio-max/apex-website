## Arabic content: the details that matter

Arabic text on video follows rules many producers miss: a larger type size than English for the same legibility, comfortable line spacing, correct right-aligned placement, and on-screen timing matched to Arabic reading speed.

The visual pacing Gulf audiences prefer also differs from Western norms. We produce in Arabic as a first language, not as subtitles laid over a video made for a different audience.
