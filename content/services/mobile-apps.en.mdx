# Mobile App Development

A mobile app is not a smaller version of your website — it is a distinct product with different performance standards and user expectations. People judge an app in the first ten seconds: launch speed, clarity of the first screen, and touch responsiveness. We build apps that pass that test.

We work with business owners across Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, and Syria, and we treat every project as a commercial product rather than a coding task: we start by understanding how the app will earn or save money, then build what delivers that.

---

## Do you actually need a mobile app?

Not every project needs one. An honest answer here can save you tens of thousands of dollars:

**You need an app if:** users return daily or weekly · you need push notifications · you need offline functionality · you need camera, location, or device sensors · your model depends on recurring subscriptions.

**You do not need an app if:** your content is purely informational · visits are seasonal or one-off · your audience finds you through search. In those cases a responsive website or a progressive web app (PWA) achieves the same outcome at a third of the cost — and we will tell you so before you spend anything.

## The technologies we work with

### Flutter
Our default choice for most projects. One codebase across iOS and Android with near-native performance and pixel-level control over the interface — which makes it excellent for custom Arabic layouts.

### React Native
A good fit when you already have a React web team or need to share business logic between web and mobile.

### Native (Swift / Kotlin)
Reserved for graphics-heavy workloads, video processing, or deep operating-system integration.

We choose the technology based on your requirements, not our preference. If the best option is something we do not specialise in, we will say so.

**1. Scoping session (no charge).** We learn your idea, audience, and revenue model, then produce a prioritised feature list and a clear scope for version one.

**2. Design and prototypes.** Clickable interfaces you can try before a line of code is written — changing a design costs hours, changing code costs weeks.

## Why an Arabic-speaking team matters for the Gulf market

Most problems in Arabic apps do not come from weak programming — they come from teams that do not use the language daily: reversed text, wrongly formatted numbers, unsupported Hijri dates, and Arabic typefaces that are unreadable on small screens.

We work in Arabic as a first language and test the app the way your customer will actually use it. Add to that Gulf time-zone alignment and direct communication with no intermediaries.
