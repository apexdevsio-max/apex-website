# AI Solutions for Business

Most AI projects fail for one reason: they start from the technology rather than the problem. A company decides it "wants AI", then goes looking for a use — and the result is a tool that impresses in a presentation and nobody uses a month later.

We work in the opposite direction: which process consumes your team's time? What question do you answer every single day? Where do you lose customers to slow replies? Then we build for that specific problem.

---

## What we actually build

### Assistants grounded in your own data
Not a generic ChatGPT window, but an assistant that knows your products, pricing, and policies and answers your customers in Arabic around the clock — escalating anything outside its knowledge to your team with the full conversation history.

### Workflow automation
Classifying and routing incoming messages, extracting data from invoices and documents, generating recurring reports, and syncing data between your existing systems.

### Document processing
Structured extraction from contracts, invoices, applications, and handwritten forms — including scanned Arabic documents.

### Data analysis and insight generation
Turning sales or customer data into readable summaries and alerts on unusual patterns.

### AI-assisted content generation
Product descriptions, marketing copy, and visual and video content — with guardrails that preserve your brand voice.

**An example from our portfolio:** we have produced AI-generated educational video series — including Tajweed lessons for children and the *Arnoub* and *Wifaq Qatar* content — combining automated generation with human direction to hold quality.

---

## Arabic: the real challenge

AI in Arabic is technically harder than in English: dialect variation, diacritics, right-to-left script, and far less specialised training data.

Practically, that means a solution built for an English audience and then translated will let you down. We test with your actual audience's dialect — Gulf, Levantine, or Egyptian — and tune responses against real examples from your customer conversations.

## How we work

**1. Diagnostic session.** We sit with your team and identify where time actually goes. Sometimes we conclude that your problem does not need AI but a process fix — and we will say so.

**2. Define the success measure.** A specific number we can track: "cut response time from 4 hours to 5 minutes", or "automate 60% of recurring enquiries".

**3. Rapid prototype.** Within two weeks you see a working version running on your real data, so you can judge the value before committing to the full project.

**4. Training and constraint.** We ground the system in your knowledge and define its boundaries: what it answers, and what it escalates to a human.

**5. Integration and measurement.** Connected to your website, WhatsApp, or internal systems, with a dashboard tracking performance against the agreed metric.

---

## What we will not promise

We will not tell you AI will double your profits or replace your team. Those promises sell projects and lose clients.

What we do promise: an honest diagnosis, a prototype you judge yourself before committing, and a straight answer when we think AI is not the right solution for you at all.
